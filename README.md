# Static WPT Charging Stations Along a Bus Line — Feasibility Study

## Overview
This project presents a technical and economic feasibility study of **static Wireless Power Transfer (WPT)** systems applied to an urban bus line.  
The main objective is to evaluate whether static charging stations — placed at bus stops or terminals — can effectively support the partial or complete electrification of a public transport line.

## Project Description
The work is divided into two main parts:
1. **Theoretical Analysis** — introduction to the principles of inductive wireless power transfer, power electronics involved, coupling efficiency, and typical design constraints for stationary systems.
2. **Feasibility Evaluation** — estimation of required power, expected energy transfer during stop times, and overall impact on vehicle autonomy and infrastructure cost.

The reference case is based on a **real bus line**, used as a benchmark to simulate charging cycles and assess the system’s practicality under realistic operating conditions.

## Goals
- Assess technical viability of static WPT for urban buses.  
- Identify key design parameters and losses.  
- Compare performance and cost against conventional plug-in charging.  
- Provide a critical discussion on real-world applicability.
